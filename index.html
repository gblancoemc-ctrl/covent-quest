    The rules, plainly

    • Walk, do not run. This is a race against the clock, not through traffic. Covent Garden is full of people and you are on foot in a tourist area. Crossing on red is a disqualification.
    • Stay on the pavement. Do not block doorways, shop entrances or bus stops.
    • Every clue is in public space — plaques, statues, signs. Nothing is inside a venue.
    • If you use a café's table or toilet, buy something. No venue is paying for this.
    • No climbing, no touching, no trespassing. Everything is reachable at eye level from the street.
    • Photos are optional. If you post one, use #CoventQuest and we may repost it with credit.
    Answer to one clue: one Covent Point. Points are earned by playing and cannot be bought. No balance, no wallet, no transfers.
